Sharing is useful when a recruiter needs a stable link to a result rather than a live owner workspace. TLDT creates a visitor-safe snapshot with its own capability token.

## What you can share

The owner workspace can share:

- a completed Chat session;
- a completed public Fast Check;
- a completed Simulate report.

The recipient sees only the safe snapshot. Depending on the resource, it can include the vacancy, Public CV context, report, transcript, or guide needed to understand the result.

## Create a share

Open the resource in **Sessions** or **Preparation**, choose **Share**, review the exact target, and confirm. Copy the generated link only after the UI shows it was created.

<Tip>
  Use a saved share when you want the recipient to review one specific conversation or simulation. Use the public username when you want an ongoing profile entry point.
</Tip>

## Revoke vs delete

**Revoke** disables the share link but keeps the source resource. It is reversible. **Delete** removes the underlying session, report, or version and is a separate destructive action.

Existing sessions keep their pinned Memory authority. Revoking a share does not silently rewrite the source or activate a newer version.
