A saved share gives another person a stable, visitor-safe view of one completed TLDT resource. Use it when a specific result is more useful than an ongoing [public profile](/public-profile/overview).

## What you can share

The owner can create a saved view for:

- a completed [public Chat session](/owner-review/sessions#review-public-chat);
- a completed [owner or public Fast Check](/public-profile/fast-check);
- a completed [Simulate report](/owner-review/simulate#save-export-clone-or-share).

The snapshot can include the vacancy, Public CV context, report, transcript, or guide needed to understand that resource. It never opens the owner workspace or exposes Private Memory, editable drafts, internal source references, prompts, or tool traces. Owner-only Memory Gap cards and saved gap answers are not part of the share.

Anyone with an active share link can view its snapshot. Review the actual transcript and report first, including personal details entered during the conversation.

## Create a share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the exact resource">
    Find the completed Chat, Fast Check, or Simulate item in [Sessions](/owner-review/sessions) or Preparation ([Fast Check](/public-profile/fast-check#run-an-owner-fast-check) or [Simulate](/owner-review/simulate#set-up-an-interview)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Share">
    Review the resource and confirm that this is the snapshot you intend to expose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the generated link">
    Share the link after creation succeeds, and open it to check the visitor view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a saved share for one stable result. Use the public username when you want a visitor to explore the active profile through Chat, Fast Check, or Public CV.
</Tip>

## Revoke, delete, and version behavior

**Revoke** disables the public link but keeps the source resource. **Delete** removes the underlying session, report, or version and is a separate destructive action.

A saved snapshot remains tied to the resource and Memory version from which it was created. Activating newer Memory does not silently rewrite the share. Saved shares have separate controls from the username page; review and revoke them separately when [removing public access](/account#remove-access-or-content).

<Note>
  A shared report is still a preparation or conversation aid. It does not prove identity, interest, availability, or a hiring outcome.
</Note>
