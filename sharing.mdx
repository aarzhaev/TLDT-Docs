A saved share gives another person a stable, visitor-safe view of one completed TLDT resource. Use it when a specific result is more useful than an ongoing public profile.

## What you can share

The owner can create a saved view for:

- a completed public Chat session;
- a completed public Fast Check;
- a completed Simulate report.

The snapshot can include the vacancy, Public CV context, report, transcript, or guide needed to understand that resource. It never opens the owner workspace or exposes Private Memory, editable drafts, source paths, prompts, policy reasons, provider metadata, or tool traces.

## Create a share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the exact resource">
    Find the completed Chat, Fast Check, or Simulate item in Sessions or Preparation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Share">
    Review the resource and confirm that this is the snapshot you intend to expose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the generated link">
    Share the link only after the UI confirms that the capability exists.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a saved share for one stable result. Use the public username when you want a visitor to explore the active profile through Chat, Fast Check, or Public CV.
</Tip>

## Revoke, delete, and version behavior

**Revoke** disables the public link but keeps the source resource. **Delete** removes the underlying session, report, or version and is a separate destructive action.

A saved snapshot remains tied to the resource and Memory authority from which it was created. Activating newer Memory does not silently rewrite the share.

<Note>
  A shared report is still a preparation or conversation aid. It does not prove identity, interest, availability, or a hiring outcome.
</Note>
