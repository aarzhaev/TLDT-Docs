Your TLDT account owns one Profile. That Profile is the authority boundary for Career Memory, published versions, preparation sessions, public activity, shares, integrations, and lifecycle actions.

## Sign in and Profile ownership

Use an email Magic Link/OTP or Google on the TLDT sign-in page. A successful sign-in provisions the owner Profile and lets you claim one public username.

Owner pages under `/admin` require authentication. A published username page and an active saved-share link are public to anyone who opens them.

Public visitors can use only the active published projection for the username or the visitor-safe snapshot addressed by the share token. They cannot choose another Profile id, storage path, or private source.

## Privacy boundary

Public serialization excludes:

- Private Memory and editable drafts;
- source paths and internal citations;
- prompts, policy reasons, and tool traces;
- provider metadata and credentials;
- tenant identifiers and internal storage authority.

Fast Check reports, simulations, and AI answers still need human judgment. Confirm important professional, legal, scheduling, and hiring decisions directly with the relevant people.

## Export and deletion

Owner export and account deletion are protected lifecycle actions. Account deletion immediately unpublishes the Profile, revokes active shares, and schedules cleanup of Profile-bound data.

The current maintenance flow can clean up Storage objects, but a downloadable export archive and complete Auth/database deletion may depend on the hosted lifecycle worker finishing its job. Treat the owner UI status as the current lifecycle state, not proof that every background step has completed.

<Warning>
  Before deletion, copy any information you need and verify the exact account. Deleting an account is separate from hiding the public profile, revoking one share, or deleting one session.
</Warning>
