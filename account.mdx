Your TLDT account owns one [Profile](/profile), including its Memory, job-search state, preparation sessions, public settings, and integrations. You choose what to retain, what to publish, and which public links to enable.

## Sign in

Use Google or the email code/sign-in link offered by TLDT. If you receive a code, enter it on the sign-in page. Use the page's resend option when the previous code or link expires. Sign out through the account menu in the sidebar.

## Private work and public access

You can search, save vacancies, practice, and improve Memory without enabling your public username page. [Search Profile](/profile#review-your-search-profile) and [Instant Fit](/jobs/instant-fit) results are private owner tools.

A draft document marked **Public** is eligible for publication; it is not live merely because you saved it. **Private** documents and editable drafts stay outside the public runtime. [Memory Loop answers](/memory/gaps#add-your-answer) remain private, and proposals need acceptance followed by a separate publication.

Published Memory is the reviewed evidence available to the twin. The **[Public link](/settings/public-link#set-the-address-and-enable-the-link)** switch determines whether visitors can open your profile. Active [saved shares](/sharing) expose selected result snapshots through their own links; review and revoke them separately.

Anyone with an active public-profile or share link can view the content that link exposes. Review transcripts before sharing them, including any personal details a participant entered.

## What visitors do not receive

Visitors cannot browse Private Memory or drafts. Public answers and snapshots exclude internal source references, prompts, tool traces, and credentials. A visible CV or permitted answer can still contain professional information you deliberately published.

Owner review does not mean independent employment verification. Confirm important claims and commitments directly with the people involved.

## Download your Memory

Use **Export** in Memory to download the selected draft or published version, including its complete documents and Properties. A draft archive can contain Private documents, so treat it as your own backup. See [Drafts and documents](/memory/drafts-and-documents#export-a-draft-or-version).

This export covers Memory, not every account record. Preparation results have their own Markdown export actions where available.

## Remove access or content

Choose the action that matches your intent:

- Turn off **[Public link](/settings/public-link#set-the-address-and-enable-the-link)** to stop access to the live username page.
- [Revoke a saved share](/sharing#revoke-delete-and-version-behavior) to disable that result link without deleting its source.
- Change the draft and [publish a new version](/memory/publish#publish-the-reviewed-version) to change the twin's active evidence.
- Delete an individual [draft, document](/memory/drafts-and-documents#revisions-deletion-and-sensitivity), or [session](/owner-review/sessions) through its own controls when you want to remove that resource.

Deleting a draft does not rewrite an immutable published version. Changing visibility in a draft does not remove material from an already-published snapshot.

Self-service full-account export and complete account erasure are not currently available. Turning off a public profile or revoking a share removes that access; it does not erase all account data.
