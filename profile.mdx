**Profile** brings together your career identity, CV, Search Profile, and public-link controls. Start with your CV to make personalized job discovery available. You can keep the public link off while you search and prepare.

## Add your CV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    In **About you**, enter your name and, optionally, your LinkedIn profile URL. Save these details. Adding a LinkedIn link does not import your LinkedIn history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a document under Your CV">
    Choose one PDF, DOC, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, Markdown, or text file, up to 2 MB. TLDT extracts the text and checks whether it is a CV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the prepared document">
    A recognized CV is prepared in Derived Memory as a Public draft candidate. It is not active yet. Check the text and Properties in Memory before publishing, especially contact details or other sensitive information.

    If TLDT cannot identify or process the CV confidently, the extracted document stays in Private Raw Memory. Use **Retry CV check** or provide a clearer document. A filename conflict asks for confirmation before using a new name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Save CV">
    When you do not yet have a published CV, **Save CV** publishes the prepared or selected CV and makes it active. It preserves currently published Memory without publishing other draft changes. TLDT then creates or updates your Search Profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also select an eligible Private document already marked as CV in your current Memory draft. Review it before **Save CV** makes it part of published Memory.

An existing published CV is read-only in Profile. To replace or retire it, edit the CV and its role in **Memory**, then [publish the reviewed version](/memory/publish).

## Review your Search Profile

Search Profile is a private description of the career evidence used for job discovery. It includes a headline, summary, roles, skills, and categories such as seniority and domains. It helps TLDT retrieve relevant vacancies; it is not your public profile or a percentage score of your employability.

It uses your active published CV and selected published Derived Memory. Drafts, Private documents, and unpublished CV candidates do not enter personalized job checks.

After [importing more career context](/memory/import-guide), publish the reviewed documents and check the Search Profile status. If **Update search profile** appears, select it. When the profile is ready, choose **Find matching jobs** or open **Job Search**.

## Choose the context

Open the **Context** tab under Search profile to inspect the source selection and context budget.

- The published CV is required.
- Click a Memory card to include or exclude it. **Select All** and **Deselect All** apply to the optional Memory documents.
- The selection applies to Search Profile and Instant Fit in both job lists and vacancy details.
- Excluding a document here does not delete it, change its visibility, or remove it from published Memory for other workflows.
- If the complete selection is too large, reduce it deliberately and update again. TLDT does not silently shorten the selected evidence.

## Keep it current

A new CV or Memory publication can make an older Search Profile out of date. Publication reports whether the Search Profile update succeeded or still needs attention. If generation fails, your CV remains published and the earlier Search Profile is preserved; retry from Profile.

When a search offers **Use previous profile**, continuing uses that version's older evidence. Choose **Update and search** to use the current selection. A previous-profile notice on a result is a reminder to interpret it against the evidence used at the time.

<Tip>
  Keep your professional examples in Memory, and use Search Profile to decide which of that published context should guide discovery. Manage visitor access separately in [Public profile settings](/settings/public-link).
</Tip>
