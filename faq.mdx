<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is TLDT a CV builder?">
    <p>It can include a Public CV, but its main purpose is a controlled conversation around your career profile. A CV is one context panel; Chat and Fast Check help a visitor ask better follow-up questions.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can visitors see my Private Memory?">
    <p>No. Public routes use the active published projection. Private files, drafts, source paths, prompts, provider metadata, policy reasons, and tool traces stay out of public responses.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does editing Memory change my public profile immediately?">
    <p>No. Draft edits remain in the owner workspace. You must preview and publish a new immutable version, then activate it to change the public runtime.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can TLDT apply for a job or negotiate for me?">
    <p>No. TLDT can explain fit and prepare a conversation. It cannot submit applications, negotiate, promise interest or availability, or make commitments on your behalf.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Fast Check and Simulate?">
    <p>Fast Check is a structured fit diagnostic that can be used from the public profile. Simulate is an owner-only interview rehearsal with a chosen vacancy, duration, HR model, and candidate response mode.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did a public answer say there is not enough information?">
    <p>The answer may be outside published Memory, outside the active disclosure boundary, or too specific for the available evidence. Add and publish a supported fact if you want TLDT to use it.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I revoke a shared result?">
    <p>Yes. Share links are separate capabilities. Revoke a link to disable that public snapshot without deleting the underlying session or report.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does voice input send a message automatically?">
    <p>No. Speech-to-text puts the returned text into the editable composer. You decide whether to edit it and press Send.</p>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
