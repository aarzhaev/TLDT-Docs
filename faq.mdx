These answers cover common decisions across the [TLDT workflow](/quickstart), from your first CV upload to improving and sharing a useful twin.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What should I do first?">
    Sign in and add your CV in Profile. If ChatGPT or Claude already knows your professional history, use Import Guide to export useful context as Markdown, upload it, review it, and publish. Check Search Profile, find a role in Job Search, then use Fast Check and Simulate to decide what to investigate and practice. Follow the [get-started walkthrough](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to make my twin public to use TLDT?">
    No. You can use published Memory for private job discovery and preparation while your Public link is off. Publishing the reviewed evidence and enabling visitor access are separate actions. Saved result shares have their own links and revocation controls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does importing connect my ChatGPT or Claude account?">
    No. Import Guide gives you a prompt to run where your assistant has relevant career context. You download its Markdown output and upload it to Derived Memory. Review the claims and Properties before publishing. TLDT does not continuously read those assistant accounts or import arbitrary chat archives automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are CV, Search Profile, and public profile different?">
    Your CV is reviewed career evidence in published Memory. Search Profile is a private description generated from that CV and selected published Derived documents for job discovery. Your public profile is the optional visitor-facing page with Chat, Fast Check, CV, and the settings you choose. See [CV and Profile](/profile).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are Instant Fit and Fast Check different?">
    [Instant Fit](/jobs/instant-fit) helps you prioritize vacancies, either across a personalized search or in one vacancy detail. [Fast Check](/public-profile/fast-check) produces a deeper report for a role you want to examine. Neither is a prediction of hiring success or a substitute for reading the job description.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need published Memory before Simulate?">
    Auto Simulate and Fast Check need usable published Memory. You can create a Manual-only simulation without it, but that session cannot later gain Auto mode or Memory-gap analysis. Publish Memory and create a new session when you want those capabilities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Manual and Auto Simulate?">
    In Manual, you write or dictate each candidate response. In Auto, the twin responds from the Memory version pinned to the session. Realistic or Diagnostic interview style is a separate choice that controls how the interviewer explores the role. An Auto answer is not your testimony.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a Memory Gap mean I lack a skill?">
    No. It means the published evidence used for that analysis did not explain something sufficiently. Read the source question and context. Supply an accurate example if you have one, or preserve the limitation if you do not. Historical findings remain tied to their original Memory version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I answer a gap without running another simulation?">
    Yes. Open its card in Memory and use **Your answer**. Type, paste text prepared elsewhere, or dictate with the microphone. Text autosaves privately; wait for **Saved**. An eligible Manual Simulate response can be copied with **Use this answer**. See [Memory Gaps](/memory/gaps).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does saving an answer update the twin?">
    No. Save the answer, choose **Generate Memory**, and review the resulting proposals. Accept the changes you want into the draft, then **Publish** to make them active. Acceptance and publication are separate. [Memory Loop](/memory/loop) explains the full process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is a gap still Open after publication?">
    Gap status is manual. Answering, accepting a proposal, or publishing does not automatically close it. Test the improved evidence, then choose Closed or Archived yourself. You can reopen it later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does every conversation improve Memory automatically?">
    No. Completed Fast Check and eligible Simulate sessions can produce gap findings, but findings do not edit Memory. Saving a transcript to Raw preserves a source. Memory Loop starts separately from your selected saved answers and produces proposals for review. Public Chat ratings do not change your career evidence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upload an audio recording into a gap?">
    The gap field records through your microphone and adds a transcription to **Your answer**. It is not an audio-file upload. For an existing recording, prepare a transcript and paste the relevant reviewed text. In Chat and Manual Simulate, voice input fills the composer and waits for you to send.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I publish new Memory?">
    Memory's **Publish** action creates and activates a fixed version of the reviewed Public draft. Search Profile reports its update status separately. Existing checks, simulations, findings, and saved shares keep their historical context; start a new check to test the new evidence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can visitors see Private documents or drafts?">
    No. Public answers use approved published Memory. A Public label on an editable draft means eligible for a later publication, not already live. Hiding the Public CV panel does not remove a CV from published Memory; change the draft and publication to change the twin's evidence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I download my Memory or revoke a shared result?">
    Yes. Memory's **Export** downloads the selected draft or published version as a ZIP of complete documents and Properties. A draft export can include Private files. Saved Chat, Fast Check, and Simulate shares can be revoked separately. Memory export is not a complete account archive; see [Account and privacy](/account).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do meetings and calendar links work?">
    Connect Cal.com and enable scheduling in Settings to allow a visible booking form in Public Chat. Visitors choose and confirm an available slot. Adding a calendar link under Profile's social links only opens that external page; it does not enable in-chat booking. See [Meetings](/settings/meetings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can TLDT apply, negotiate, or commit for me?">
    No. It can help you discover roles, explain published evidence, practice, and offer configured meeting booking. Applications, career decisions, interest, compensation, and other commitments remain yours. Simulate is preparation, not a hidden realtime interview copilot.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
