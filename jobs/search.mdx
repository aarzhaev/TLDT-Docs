Job Search helps you find a real role to evaluate and prepare for. You can browse the [public catalogue](https://tldt.me/jobs) before signing in. Open **Job Discovery → Job Search** after signing in to use vacancy details, bookmarks, recent history, saved searches, and personalized checks.

## Search and filter

Enter a role, skill, company, or other useful keyword. Use **Filters** to narrow the catalogue by the available role, location, work arrangement, pay, benefits, and eligibility fields.

Filter choices have three states:

1. Click once to include a value.
2. Click again to exclude it.
3. Click a third time to clear that choice.

Apply the filter dialog to run the search. The chips below the search field show the active choices; remove a chip to remove that filter. Location search covers regions, countries, cities, and metro areas.

Use the posting-date window and sort controls to focus on recent roles. **Posted** dates refer to the vacancy's source; **in catalogue** dates refer to when it entered the catalogue. They can differ. You can switch the date shown on cards by clicking the age label.

Search results may show an English synopsis or the original description. Open the vacancy to read its full details and inspect the company context when available. Use the original posting to verify whether the role is still open and how to apply.

## Save a search you will repeat

Use the plus control beside the search views to save the current search with a name. Opening that named view restores its query and filters. After changing a named search, use its save action to update it explicitly. You can rename or delete a saved search.

A saved search keeps search settings. A [saved vacancy](/jobs/saved-vacancies) keeps a specific role. Saving a search does not subscribe you to vacancy alerts or apply for jobs.

## Personalize with Instant Fit

After setting up your [CV and Search Profile](/profile), turn on **Instant Fit** beside Filters. TLDT retrieves a bounded set of relevant roles and checks them against your selected published evidence. Your explicit query and filters still constrain the search.

Read the [Instant Fit guide](/jobs/instant-fit) for the result labels, source selection, and recovery options.

## Take a vacancy into preparation

From a vacancy detail page, choose **Fast Check** for a deeper fit report or **Simulate** for an interview. The role is carried into the new workflow, so you do not need to paste it again. These actions use a saved copy of the vacancy, keeping that session's role context stable even if the original posting changes later.

You can also start either workflow directly with a vacancy link, pasted description, or a saved vacancy. TLDT does not submit applications; use the employer's original application route when you decide to apply.

## If search is interrupted

Keep the query and filters, read the error, and use the offered retry. A search-limit message includes when you can try again. Missing results or unavailable live counts are not evidence that no suitable role exists.
