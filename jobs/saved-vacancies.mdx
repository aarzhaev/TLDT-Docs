Saved Vacancies keeps roles you want to revisit without repeating the search. Bookmark a vacancy from a result card or its detail page, then open **Job Discovery → Saved Vacancies**.

## Saved and recently viewed

In Job Search, **Saved** and **Recently viewed** open your own collections. The dedicated Saved Vacancies sidebar page focuses on bookmarked roles. Recently viewed history records roles you have opened; it is separate from your bookmarks.

Remove a bookmark when you no longer want the role in Saved. Removing a role from recent history does not remove an existing bookmark. Neither action deletes an already-created Fast Check or Simulate session.

## Why a vacancy has a Snapshot badge

TLDT retains a copy of the vacancy when it is saved or used in an owner workflow. That copy can remain available after the original listing changes or disappears.

Use **Update** on the **Snapshot** badge to request the current listing. A successful refresh creates a new saved copy. Existing Fast Check and Simulate sessions keep the exact vacancy version they started with.

**Outdated** means the current listing could not be retrieved. The stored role remains available for reference and preparation; it does not establish that the job is still accepting applications.

## Reuse a role

Open the saved vacancy and choose **Fast Check** or **Simulate**. You can also choose a saved vacancy when creating a preparation session. Check the role description before starting, particularly if you are using an older snapshot.

Vacancy copies are role context. Saving or updating one does not add it to your personal Career Memory or turn an employer's requirements into facts about you.

<Tip>
  Save a search for a recurring set of filters; save a vacancy for a particular opportunity. Use the original posting to confirm availability and submit an application yourself.
</Tip>
