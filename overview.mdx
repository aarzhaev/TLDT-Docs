TLDT is a candidate-owned AI career profile. It gives recruiters a clear way to understand your experience before the first call, while you keep control over what the profile can say.

<Frame caption="The TLDT profile workflow"><img src="/images/profile-loop.svg" alt="A diagram showing Memory, a shared profile, and the next step for a recruiter" /></Frame>

## What TLDT helps you do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Build a trusted profile" icon="brain" href="/memory/overview">
    Organize your own evidence into Memory, review it, and publish only the version you approve.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Give recruiters useful context" icon="message-circle" href="/public-profile/chat">
    Let visitors ask about your experience through a public Chat that uses your published Memory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check fit for a role" icon="briefcase-business" href="/public-profile/fast-check">
    Run a structured Fast Check against a job description without turning it into an automated hiring decision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Practice before the call" icon="clipboard-check" href="/owner-review/simulate">
    Run an owner-only HR simulation, then review the transcript, strengths, gaps, and missing evidence.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choose your path

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="I am a profile owner">
    Start with [Quick Start](/quickstart), then add Memory and publish your public link. The owner workspace contains Memory, Preview, Preparation, Sessions, and Settings.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I received a TLDT link">
    Open the link and use Chat, Fast Check, or the saved view that the owner shared. Read [Public profile](/public-profile/overview) to understand what each action does.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>TLDT represents the owner from reviewed, published context. It can explain experience, but it cannot invent facts, make commitments, or act as the person.</Tip>
