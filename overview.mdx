TLDT helps you use reviewed career evidence for a real vacancy or interview. Check how your experience maps to the role, rehearse the conversation, and test what your published AI profile can answer without inventing facts.

Career Memory is the reusable asset behind these workflows. You decide what enters it, what stays private, and which reviewed version becomes available to Fast Check, Simulate, and an optional public profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare a small, accurate Career Memory">
    Add evidence you trust, review visibility and sensitivity, then publish and activate a usable version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring a real role">
    Run Fast Check to separate supported fit, partial fit, real gaps, and missing proof.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rehearse or test">
    Use Manual Simulate to practice your own answers, or Auto Simulate to test how the published AI profile represents you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and reuse">
    Keep useful evidence under your control. Saving a session to Raw Memory is always an explicit owner action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share only when useful">
    Optionally let a recruiter explore the same reviewed context through Public Chat, Public CV, Fast Check, or a saved snapshot.
  </Step>
</Steps>
