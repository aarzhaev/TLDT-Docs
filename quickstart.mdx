Start with the career information you already have. This walkthrough takes you through one complete TLDT cycle: add evidence, find a role, test it, answer a gap, and improve the Memory you will reuse next time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and upload your CV">
    Open [TLDT](https://tldt.me), [sign up or log in](/account#sign-in) with Google or the email code/link offered on the page, then open **[Profile](/profile#add-your-cv)**. Add your name and upload your CV under **Your CV**.

    Review the prepared document and choose **Save CV** when you are ready to publish it. This makes the CV active and starts preparing your Search Profile. See [CV and Profile](/profile) for supported formats and how to replace an existing CV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in the career context your assistant already knows">
    If you have discussed your work with ChatGPT or Claude, the quickest way to bootstrap richer Memory is **[Import Guide](/memory/import-guide)** in the sidebar. Choose **Open in ChatGPT**, **Open in Claude**, or **Copy prompt**.

    Run the prompt where the assistant has your professional context. Save the Markdown files it produces and upload them in the guide. They enter **[Derived Memory](/memory/drafts-and-documents#raw-and-derived-serve-different-jobs)** for review. This is a prompt-assisted export and upload, not an automatic account connection.

    If the assistant has little useful context, start with your CV and your own project notes. You can fill specific gaps later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish your initial Memory">
    Open **[Memory](/memory/overview#the-memory-workspace)**. Check the imported text and [Properties](/memory/drafts-and-documents#documents-and-properties), including visibility, sensitivity, and the [CV, Soul, Boundaries, and Voice roles](/memory/overview#special-roles). Correct unsupported claims and mark only reviewed documents **Public**.

    Choose **[Publish](/memory/publish#publish-the-reviewed-version)**, inspect the complete proposed version, and confirm. This creates and activates the new Memory version. Your **[Public link](/settings/public-link#set-the-address-and-enable-the-link)** can remain off while you use it for private preparation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Search Profile and find relevant jobs">
    In **[Profile → Search profile](/profile#review-your-search-profile)**, check that the profile is up to date. Use **Update search profile** if needed. The **[Context](/profile#choose-the-context)** tab lets you choose which published Memory documents accompany the required CV.

    Open **[Job Search](/jobs/search#search-and-filter)**, enter your query and filters, and turn on **[Instant Fit](/jobs/instant-fit#check-a-list-of-jobs)** for personalized results. Read promising vacancies and [bookmark those you want to revisit](/jobs/saved-vacancies). You can also bring a vacancy you found elsewhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Fast Check on one promising vacancy">
    From the vacancy detail choose **[Fast Check](/public-profile/fast-check#run-an-owner-fast-check)**, or start one from **Preparation → Fast Check** using a [saved vacancy](/jobs/saved-vacancies#reuse-a-role), link, or pasted description. Review the role and run the check.

    Read the strengths, partial fit, mismatches, missing proof, and questions to validate. The report helps you decide what to investigate or rehearse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Simulate to test your twin and practice">
    Create a **[Simulate](/owner-review/simulate#set-up-an-interview)** session for that role. **[Auto](/owner-review/simulate#choose-who-answers)** shows what the twin can answer from its pinned published Memory. Review the report and its **[Memory Gaps](/memory/gaps#where-gaps-come-from)** tab to see what needs more evidence.

    Use **[Manual](/owner-review/simulate#choose-who-answers)** when you want to answer the interviewer yourself. You can practice without first running Auto; the two modes serve different purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the missing questions">
    Open a gap in **[Memory](/memory/gaps)**. Under **[Your answer](/memory/gaps#add-your-answer)**, type, paste text written elsewhere, or dictate with the microphone. Text saves automatically; wait for **Saved**.

    If a gap includes your own Manual Simulate response, **Use this answer** copies that complete saved answer into the field. Auto answers cannot become your testimony through this action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Memory update">
    Choose **[Generate Memory](/memory/loop#generate-proposed-changes)**, select the saved answers, and run generation. Open the **[Review](/memory/loop#review-in-derived-memory)** cards in Derived Memory, compare proposed updates, edit when needed, then accept or reject.

    Acceptance changes the draft only. Use **[Publish](/memory/publish#publish-the-reviewed-version)** to make the reviewed improvements active. Check [Search Profile](/profile#keep-it-current) afterward, test the missing question again, and [close the gap yourself](/memory/gaps#manage-status-yourself) when it is resolved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your twin public when it is useful">
    Test representative questions in **[Preview](/owner-review/preview)**. In **[Profile](/settings/public-link)**, set your username, public copy, avatar, links, and visitor options, then enable the **Public link** and save.

    Optionally [connect Cal.com](/settings/meetings) in **Settings** for visible meeting offers. Share your profile or a [selected completed result](/sharing#create-a-share) with someone who needs it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You do not need a complete career archive to start. Repeat the answer, review, and publication steps whenever a real vacancy or conversation reveals something worth preserving.
