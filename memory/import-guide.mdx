Open **Memory → Import Guide** before your first upload. It is the in-product checklist for turning your existing career material into a reviewable TLDT workspace.

## What to import first

Start with the smallest set that covers the conversations you expect:

- your current CV or résumé;
- project notes with dates, outcomes, and your role;
- interview transcripts or work samples you own;
- a short statement of preferred roles, working style, and boundaries;
- links or documents that help you explain a specific project.

Keep unrelated sources in separate files. That makes review and retrieval easier.

## Upload flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a draft">
    Select the workspace you want to update. You can create an empty draft, copy another draft, or copy an immutable published version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Raw files">
    Upload Markdown or text through the Raw column. TLDT stores the content separately from its Properties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Properties analysis">
    TLDT can propose a title, summary, topics, and use-when hint with the selected configured model. Treat the proposal as a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each file">
    Confirm visibility, sensitivity, source details, and any role flags. Change the content when the evidence needs correction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Never use generation to fill an evidence gap. If a source does not support a claim, keep the gap visible or add a source that you own and can review.</Warning>

## Import checklist

Before publication, check that every personal claim has an owner-supplied source, team outcomes are not presented as individual ownership, dates and metrics are exact, and Public/Private choices are intentional.
