Career Memory preserves the context behind your CV: projects, decisions, responsibilities, outcomes, constraints, working style, and examples. You can reuse that evidence across job searches, interviews, and an optional public twin instead of rebuilding your story each time.

## Start with what you already have

Add your [CV in Profile](/profile). If ChatGPT or Claude already has useful career context, follow [Import Guide](/memory/import-guide) to produce reviewable Memory files. You can also [upload source documents](/memory/import-guide#if-you-are-starting-with-documents) or [write your own notes](/memory/drafts-and-documents#draft-workspaces).

## The Memory workspace

Open **Memory** from the sidebar. Its three areas work together:

| Area | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **[Memory Gaps](/memory/gaps)** | Review missing-evidence questions and save your own answers in text or by voice. |
| **[Raw Memory](/memory/drafts-and-documents#raw-and-derived-serve-different-jobs)** | Keep original or extracted evidence: notes, documents, transcripts, and saved answers. |
| **[Derived Memory](/memory/drafts-and-documents#raw-and-derived-serve-different-jobs)** | Maintain useful career explanations and review proposed changes from [Memory Loop](/memory/loop#review-in-derived-memory). |

On smaller screens, select the area you want to work in. Opening a document lets you read and edit its full content and **[Properties](/memory/drafts-and-documents#documents-and-properties)**. Draft and version controls separate the workspace you are editing from the published snapshot used by the twin.

## Two ways to build Derived Memory

- **From existing sources:** upload reviewed `.md` files from Import Guide, create a card yourself, or [generate a candidate from selected Raw documents](/memory/generate-derived).
- **From new answers:** [answer a gap](/memory/gaps#add-your-answer), choose **Generate Memory**, and [review the proposed additions or updates](/memory/loop).

Both paths require your review. Ordinary Raw-to-Derived generation starts with a Private candidate. Memory Loop's **Accept for publication** creates a Public draft card, while updates preserve the target's visibility. Neither generation nor acceptance publishes a version.

## Special roles

A draft can have at most one Public document for each special role:

| Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| CV | Your reviewed career summary, also used by Search Profile. |
| Soul | Identity and stance for how the twin represents you. |
| Boundaries | Disclosure limits and topics that need care. |
| Voice | Speaking style and representative professional language. |

Roles are selected in Properties. A filename alone does not assign a role. Soul, Boundaries, and Voice guide representation; they do not establish work experience.

## Review, publish, reuse

**Public** on a draft card means eligible for the next publication. **Private** keeps a document out of published runtime Memory. Choose [Publish](/memory/publish) to review the full Public draft and make a new version active.

[Search Profile](/profile#choose-the-context) uses the published CV and selected Derived context. [Fast Check](/public-profile/fast-check), [Preview](/owner-review/preview), [Public Chat](/public-profile/chat), and [Auto Simulate](/owner-review/simulate#choose-who-answers) use published evidence under their own workflow rules. Existing sessions keep their original Memory version.

When a real question exposes missing detail, return to the gap, answer it, review the proposed change, and publish. Keep the claim precise: your role rather than the whole team's work, the actual result rather than a stronger estimate, and uncertainty where it belongs.

Use **[Export](/memory/drafts-and-documents#export-a-draft-or-version)** to download the selected draft or version. See [Drafts and documents](/memory/drafts-and-documents) for workspace management and backups.
