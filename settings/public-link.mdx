Public profile settings control the optional recruiter-facing representation built from your active published Career Memory.

Open **Settings** from the owner sidebar after you have reviewed the profile through Owner Preview and, for a role-specific test, Auto Simulate.

## Public profile address and status

Choose the username that forms your address, for example `tldt.me/alex`. The profile can be enabled only when the username and an active published Memory version are ready.

Use **Copy link** to share the address or **Public page** to inspect it yourself.

## Visitor options

- **Visitor validation:** require a short visitor form before Chat continues, or leave the introduction optional.
- **Allow interpretation:** permit clearly labeled interpretation and general knowledge in addition to grounded published Memory.
- **Public CV:** show or hide the owner-approved CV panel.
- **Starter prompts:** edit the question chips above the public Chat input.

Disabling interpretation keeps personal answers within published Memory and disclosure policy. No setting exposes Private Memory or editable drafts.

## Public copy and languages

Edit the header, welcome title, description, and starter prompts separately for English and Russian. The public language switch shows available localized copy.

Describe what a visitor can ask and what the profile can support. Do not imply that the AI can accept roles, negotiate, promise interest or availability, or make decisions for you.

## Models and routing

When BYOK is enabled, manage the visible model allowlist and choose price- or throughput-oriented provider routing. Visitors see only the effective public model list. Provider details and credentials remain owner-only.

## Social links and scheduling

Add supported HTTPS social links and, when configured, a Cal.com destination. Scheduling remains a visible, bounded flow; the conversational AI cannot create a hidden commitment.

<Warning>
  Enabling the public profile is a publication decision. Test the active version first and confirm that every public document, special role, language, and link matches your intent.
</Warning>
