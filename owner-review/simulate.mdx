Simulate is an owner-only interview preparation workflow for one real vacancy. It uses the same role context as Fast Check but answers a different question: how will the interview conversation unfold?

## Choose the candidate response mode

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manual — How would I answer?" icon="user">
    You write every candidate response. Use Manual mode to rehearse examples, phrasing, and honest boundaries in your own voice.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Auto — What can my AI profile answer?" icon="robot">
    TLDT answers from the published Career Memory version pinned to the session. Use Auto mode to test the quality and limits of your approved representation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Manual and Auto are not better and worse versions of the same mode. They test different subjects: your live answer and the published profile's answer.

## Create a simulation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preparation → Simulate">
    Create a simulation and paste one job link or vacancy description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the vacancy">
    Confirm the extracted role, company, location, work model, specialization, and description when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the setup">
    Select **20** or **40 minutes**, an HR model, a TLDT model, a language, and an interview style: **Realistic** or **Diagnostic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Manual or Auto">
    Select **Manual** to answer yourself or **Auto** to let the pinned AI profile answer. A session created without active published Memory remains Manual-only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run, pause, and resume">
    Start the interview and continue until the guide is complete. Pausing preserves the session so you can return later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the report">
    Inspect the transcript, verdict, strengths, concerns, missing evidence, and questions to validate. Treat the report as preparation, not an outcome prediction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Realistic vs Diagnostic

| Interview style | Best for | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Realistic | Rehearsing the shape and pace of a first screen. | HR follows the guide naturally and may finish after the important requirements and gaps are covered. |
| Diagnostic | Stress-testing evidence coverage. | HR probes more of the guide and follows gaps more strictly. |

Interview style controls HR behavior. Manual or Auto controls who writes the candidate answer.

## Memory and session boundaries

A simulation pins its published Memory version when the session is created. Later publication does not rewrite the interview or report.

You may explicitly save the vacancy, interviewer, transcript, and report to Raw Memory. This preserves a source for later review. It does not automatically extract a Memory Delta, change Derived Memory, compare Manual and Auto answers, or publish new facts.

A completed simulation can also become a visitor-safe saved share when you want another person to review the exact snapshot.

<Warning>
  Simulate is not a live interview copilot and does not supply hidden real-time answers during an actual interview.
</Warning>
