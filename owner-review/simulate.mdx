Simulate is a private interview-preparation workflow for one vacancy. An AI interviewer asks role-specific questions and follow-ups. You can answer yourself or let the twin answer from published Memory, then review the transcript, diagnostic report, and Memory Gaps.

## Choose who answers

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manual: practice your answers" icon="user">
    Type or dictate your response, review the text, and send it. Use your own examples to rehearse how you explain your work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto: test your twin" icon="robot">
    TLDT answers from the published Memory pinned to the session. Use it to see what your representation can explain and where evidence is missing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can switch modes in an eligible session. Each answer retains its recorded author, so an Auto answer does not become your testimony merely because you later switch to Manual.

A session created without active published Memory stays **Manual-only**. Publish Memory and create a new session to use Auto and Memory-gap analysis.

## Set up an interview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a real vacancy">
    Open **Preparation → Simulate**, use a saved vacancy, or paste a job link or description. You can also launch Simulate directly from a Job Search vacancy detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze and review the vacancy">
    Check the extracted role and requirements. Correct missing or inaccurate source text before generating the interview guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the setup and generate the guide">
    Select **20** or **40 minutes**, the available HR and TLDT models, language, and **Realistic** or **Diagnostic** style. Review the generated guide. Changes to guide-defining settings may require generating it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in Manual or Auto">
    Manual waits for your candidate responses. Auto continues with the twin answering. Use **Pause** and **Resume** to control the run; saved sessions remain available in the history list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Inspect the transcript and report for strengths, concerns, evidence gaps, and questions to validate. Open **Memory Gaps** for findings tied to this session's evidence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Interview style | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Realistic | A natural first-screen conversation that can finish once important requirements and gaps are covered. |
| Diagnostic | More systematic probing of evidence coverage and the interview guide. |

Style controls the interviewer. Manual or Auto controls the candidate response.

## Voice and assisted drafting

In Manual mode, the microphone transcribes into the editable answer. Review it and send it yourself. Recording is speech-to-text, not a live voice conversation.

When the session has published Memory, **Generate LLM response** can prepare a candidate draft for review. Check every claim before using it. Generating a draft is not evidence that you personally supplied or verified a new fact.

## Improve Memory after practice

1. Open a finding in the report's **Memory Gaps** tab or the Memory workspace.
2. Review the question and its source. If it includes an eligible answer you gave in Manual mode, use **Use this answer** to copy the complete saved response.
3. Add missing detail under **Your answer**, or dictate a fresh explanation without running another simulation.
4. Use [Generate Memory and Review](/memory/loop) to prepare changes, then accept and publish only what you approve.

An Auto run is useful for exposing what the twin cannot yet explain. Your own answer supplies the evidence needed to improve it. Gaps remain Open until you explicitly change their status.

## Save, export, clone, or share

The simulation action menu includes:

- **Clone** to create another preparation session from an existing setup.
- **Export .md** to download the simulation as Markdown.
- **Save to Memory** to preserve the vacancy, interviewer, transcript, and report in Raw Memory.
- **Share** for an eligible completed visitor-safe snapshot, with a separate revoke action.
- **Delete** to remove the source session.

Saving the full transcript is different from Memory Loop: it preserves a source without proposing or applying Derived changes. Review session content before publishing any material derived from it.

Existing simulations retain their original Memory version after you publish improvements. Create a new simulation to test the new version.

<Note>
  Reports support preparation; they do not predict a real hiring outcome. Simulate is not a hidden live interview copilot and does not submit applications or make commitments for you.
</Note>
