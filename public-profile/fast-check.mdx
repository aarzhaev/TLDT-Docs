## title: "Fast Check" description: "Run a structured, evidence-aware fit check against a vacancy from a public TLDT profile."

Fast Check is a separate diagnostic from Chat. It helps a visitor decide whether a first conversation is worth having and shows where the published profile has strong evidence or open questions.

## Run a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fast Check">
    Choose the Fast Check tab on the public profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one vacancy">
    Paste a job link or the vacancy text. Use one substantive role at a time so the result has a clear target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the fit report">
    Read the summary, strengths, gaps, missing evidence, and questions to validate. The report is grounded in the published profile version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the result as a conversation aid">
    Open the Public CV or Chat to explore a promising area. A missing fact is a reason to ask a question, not proof of a poor fit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the report means

Fast Check can highlight evidence, partial coverage, gaps, and questions. It is not an automated hiring decision and it does not validate your identity or require a meeting.

If the profile lacks evidence, the report does not turn that absence into a definitive **no fit**. Treat the output as a structured starting point for a human conversation.

## Share a completed check

The owner can create a visitor-safe share of a completed Fast Check. A share is a snapshot: revoking it disables that link, while the source session remains a separate owner resource.
