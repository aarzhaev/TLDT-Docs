title: "Chat"
description: "Ask a TLDT profile about the owner's published experience and prepare a better first conversation."
---

Chat is the main visitor conversation. It answers from the active published Memory and follows the owner's disclosure boundaries.

## Ask useful questions

Good questions are specific and connected to a real conversation:

- “Which product decisions did you own in your last role?”
- “Tell me about a project where the constraints changed.”
- “How does this experience relate to this role?”
- “What would you want to clarify before a first call?”

When exact evidence is unavailable, TLDT may say so. If interpretation is enabled, a useful inference can be labeled as **interpretation**; non-personal context can be labeled **world knowledge**.

## Visitor validation

The owner can require a short form before Chat continues. Depending on the profile settings, it can ask for your name, work email, role, purpose, company or context, a job link, LinkedIn, and a preferred messenger.

The form helps the owner understand who is starting the conversation. Your submitted context remains untrusted context; it does not grant access to Private Memory or make you a hiring decision-maker.

If validation is disabled, Chat can remain available without the form. The page may offer an optional introduction instead.

## Voice input

Use the voice control when it is available. TLDT transcribes the recording into the editable composer. Review the text, make changes if needed, and press **Send** yourself. Recording never auto-sends a message.

## Meetings

Chat may offer a meeting form only after the conversation has a concrete purpose and the owner's settings allow scheduling. The form, slot, and final booking go through separate checks. TLDT cannot promise availability, accept a role, negotiate, or book outside the visible flow.

<Note>Chat is a representation of the owner’s reviewed profile, not the physical person. Use it to qualify a conversation; confirm important details with the owner.</Note>
