Public Chat lets a visitor explore the owner's active published [Career Memory](/memory/overview). It is useful after the owner chooses to [share a public representation](/public-profile/overview); it is not the starting point for building or privately testing Memory.

## Ask questions that improve a real conversation

Useful questions connect evidence to a concrete need:

- “Which experience supports this requirement?”
- “What did you personally own in this project?”
- “What constraints changed the decision?”
- “How does this experience relate to this role?”
- “What is not covered by the published profile?”
- “What should we clarify with the owner on a first call?”

When exact evidence is unavailable, TLDT may say so. Answer labels explain the basis of a response:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Grounded** | Supported by published owner-approved Memory, or states a boundary without adding an unsupported personal claim. |
| **Interpretation** | An inference from related approved evidence. Confirm it with the owner. |
| **General knowledge** | General knowledge or public research, rather than evidence about the owner. |
| **Not labeled** | A saved answer has no recorded label; its basis cannot be determined from that record. |

The owner [controls whether interpretation is allowed](/settings/public-link#choose-visitor-options). One answer can contain sections with different labels. These labels describe the basis of the text, not a guarantee that it is correct.

## Give feedback on an answer

Use the thumbs-up or thumbs-down control below a twin reply. Choose the selected icon again to clear your rating, or choose the other icon to change it. The owner can review this feedback alongside the conversation in [Sessions](/owner-review/sessions).

A rating does not edit Memory or verify a factual claim. Ask a follow-up question when you need a missing detail.

## Visitor context and validation

The owner can [require a short form](/settings/public-link#choose-visitor-options) before Chat continues. Depending on settings, it may ask for a visitor's name, work email, role, purpose, company or context, job link, LinkedIn, and preferred messenger.

Visitor input remains untrusted context. It does not grant access to Private Memory, change the profile, or give the visitor decision-making authority. When validation is disabled, Chat may remain available without the form and offer an optional introduction instead.

## Voice input

When available, voice input transcribes a short recording into the editable composer. The visitor reviews the text and presses **Send**. Recording never sends a message automatically and TLDT does not provide a real-time voice representative.

## Meetings and commitments

Chat may offer a visible [Cal.com meeting form](/settings/meetings#what-a-visitor-does) when the conversation has a concrete purpose, you agree to proceed, and the owner's settings allow scheduling. Choose a duration and available slot, enter the requested contact details, and confirm. A meeting offer is not a booking; wait for the successful booking confirmation.

TLDT cannot promise that the owner is interested, available, willing to relocate, accepting a role, or agreeing to compensation or other terms. Confirm important details directly with the owner.

<Note>
  Public Chat represents reviewed published context. It is not the physical person, a recruiter, or an autonomous agent authorized to act for either side.
</Note>
