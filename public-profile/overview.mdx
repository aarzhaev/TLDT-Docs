Public representation is an optional extension of [Career Memory](/memory/overview). After you have [reviewed and published useful professional context](/memory/publish), you can let a recruiter or another visitor explore a bounded version of the same evidence.

A public profile lives at `tldt.me/your-username`. The owner chooses whether it is enabled, which Memory version is active, which public features are visible, and what introductory copy a visitor sees.

## What a visitor can use

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Public Chat" icon="comment-dots" href="/public-profile/chat">
    Ask about approved experience, projects, working style, or a role.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fast Check" icon="briefcase" href="/public-profile/fast-check">
    Compare one vacancy with published evidence and identify questions to validate.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Public CV" icon="file-lines" href="/settings/public-link#choose-visitor-options">
    Read the owner-approved CV when the owner enables the panel.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Saved views" icon="share-nodes" href="/sharing">
    Open a visitor-safe snapshot of a completed Chat, Fast Check, or Simulate resource.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A relevant conversation may also offer a visible [Cal.com booking form](/settings/meetings) when the owner has connected scheduling and enabled it.

Set the username, avatar, public copy, visitor options, and professional links in **Profile**. See [Public profile settings](/settings/public-link). Publishing Memory does not turn on the public link automatically.

## What remains under owner control

- editable drafts and Private Memory stay in the owner workspace;
- the public runtime reads only the active immutable version;
- the owner controls visitor validation, interpretation, Public CV, starter prompts, models, languages, and social links;
- a saved share can be [revoked](/sharing#revoke-delete-and-version-behavior) without deleting its source;
- important decisions and commitments remain with the owner.

## Representation is not delegated authority

The AI profile can explain reviewed context and state when evidence is missing. It cannot apply, accept or reject a role, negotiate, promise interest or availability, or make other commitments for the owner.

<Tip>
  Use [Owner Preview](/owner-review/preview) before enabling the link. Use [Auto Simulate](/owner-review/simulate#choose-who-answers) when you want to test the representation against a specific vacancy and interview guide.
</Tip>
